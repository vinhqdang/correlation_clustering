\begin{abstract}
Cluster Editing asks for the fewest edge insertions and deletions that turn a graph into a disjoint union of cliques.
Li, Pilipczuk and Sorge parameterize it by the excess~$\ell$ of the solution size over the size of a modification-disjoint packing~$H$ of induced $P_3$'s, and ask for which packings the problem is fixed-parameter tractable.
We use the treewidth~$w$ of the intersection graph $I(H)$ of the packing as the structural measure.
Our main result is negative: the problem is W[1]-hard parameterized by $\ell+w$, also by $\ell+\mathrm{pw}(I(H))$ and by $\ell+c$, where $c$ is the maximum number of packed $P_3$'s through a vertex, even for pair-maximal packings. So there is no algorithm in time $f(\ell,w)n^{O(1)}$ unless $\mathrm{FPT}=\mathrm{W[1]}$, and under ETH none in time $f(\ell,w)n^{o(\sqrt\ell)}$.
The reduction, from Grid Tiling with $\le$, is computer-assisted at four finite steps, which we list; its counting core and several structural lemmas are also machine-checked in Lean~4, which does not cover the graph-level statements of the reduction.
Matching upper bounds come from a constraint-satisfaction view: time $3^{w+1}n^{O(1)}$ for $\ell=0$ and $5^{w+1}n^{2\ell+O(1)}$ in general; under ETH there is no $2^{o(w)}n^{O(1)}$ algorithm even for $\ell=0$.
We isolate a configuration, the soft triangle, as the source of difficulty: without soft triangles the case $\ell=0$ is polynomial for every $c$ and $w$.
Further structural results and the open questions are stated, with proofs in the appendices.
