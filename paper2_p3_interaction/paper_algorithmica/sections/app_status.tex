\section{Scope and status of the results}\label{app:status}
The following table summarises the scope and status of the results. ``Input $H$'' means that the statement is in terms of $w$, $\ell$ and $n$ of the input packing; ``pair-maximal $H'$'' means that it is stated for a pair-maximal packing, and for an arbitrary input applies to a pair-maximal extension $H'$ with the primed parameters of Section~\ref{sec:prelim}. ``YES'' and ``optimal'' mean that the statement needs a YES instance, respectively an optimal solution.
