# Highlights

- Cluster Editing above a modification-disjoint P3 packing is W[1]-hard parameterized by the excess l plus the treewidth of the packing's intersection graph, and also by l plus pathwidth or l plus the maximum number of packed P3's through a vertex.
- Under ETH there is no f(l,w) n^{o(sqrt l)} algorithm; the reduction is from Grid Tiling with <=.
- A CSP formulation gives 3^{w+1} n^{O(1)} for l = 0 and 5^{w+1} n^{2l+O(1)} in general; no 2^{o(w)} n^{O(1)} algorithm exists under ETH even at l = 0.
- Soft triangles are the source of hardness: without them l = 0 is polynomial.
- Computer-assisted steps are listed; a counting core is checked in Lean 4.
