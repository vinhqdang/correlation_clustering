\title[Cluster Editing above a $P_3$ packing]{Cluster Editing above a $P_3$ packing: W[1]-hardness in the excess and the treewidth of the intersection graph}

\author*[1]{\fnm{Quang-Vinh} \sur{Dang}}\email{vinh.dq4@buv.edu.vn}

\affil*[1]{\orgname{British University Vietnam}, \orgaddress{\city{Hung Yen}, \country{Vietnam}}; ORCID: \href{https://orcid.org/0000-0002-3877-8024}{0000-0002-3877-8024}}

\abstract{Cluster Editing asks for the fewest edge insertions and deletions that turn a graph into a disjoint union of cliques.
A known parameterization measures the solution size above the size of a modification-disjoint packing~$H$ of induced paths on three vertices, called the excess~$\ell$, and asks for which packings the problem is fixed-parameter tractable.
We use the treewidth~$w$ of the intersection graph of the packing as the structural measure.
Our main result is negative: the problem is W[1]-hard parameterized by $\ell+w$, also by $\ell$ plus the pathwidth of the intersection graph, and by $\ell+c$, where $c$ is the maximum number of packed paths through a vertex, even for pair-maximal packings.
Hence there is no algorithm with running time $f(\ell,w)n^{O(1)}$ unless fixed-parameter tractability collapses to W[1], and under the Exponential Time Hypothesis none with running time $f(\ell,w)n^{o(\sqrt\ell)}$.
The reduction, from Grid Tiling with $\le$, is computer-assisted at four finite steps, which we list; its counting core and several structural lemmas are also machine-checked in the proof assistant Lean~4, which does not cover the graph-level statements of the reduction.
Matching upper bounds come from a constraint-satisfaction view: time $3^{w+1}n^{O(1)}$ for $\ell=0$ and $5^{w+1}n^{2\ell+O(1)}$ in general; under the Exponential Time Hypothesis there is no $2^{o(w)}n^{O(1)}$ algorithm even for $\ell=0$.
We isolate a configuration, the soft triangle, as the source of difficulty: without soft triangles the case $\ell=0$ is polynomial for every $c$ and $w$.
Further structural results and open questions are stated, with proofs in the appendices.}

\keywords{Cluster Editing, Parameterized complexity, W[1]-hardness, Treewidth, Packing of induced paths, Constraint satisfaction}
