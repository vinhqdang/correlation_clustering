\section{Computational checks}\label{app:checks}
The proofs in this paper do not depend on computation, except (i) that the statements about explicit small instances are verified by exhaustive enumeration, as indicated where they occur, (ii) the finite classification steps marked \emph{by computer} in Section~\ref{sec:soft}, and (iii) the four computer-assisted steps (CA1)--(CA4) in the proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:hard} (the chain lemma and the transfer-matrix certificate, the host profiles, and the counts $u_{\max}\le3$, $c\le4p+2$), listed in Section~\ref{sec:hardcomp} and Appendix~\ref{app:hardness}. This appendix lists what was enumerated and what was tested.

\paragraph{Exhaustive enumeration of explicit instances.}
For each of the following instances all set partitions of the vertex set were enumerated and the cost of each partition computed: the instances of Remark~\ref{rem:domain} (domain size), Propositions~\ref{prop:vertexmax} and~\ref{prop:c4}, the shrink-rule example of Appendix~\ref{app:structure} (for twin classes of size $1,\dots,4$), the three instances of Proposition~\ref{prop:cex}, the family of Proposition~\ref{prop:FAR} for $m=3,4,5$, the two instances of Proposition~\ref{prop:NFbad}, the gadget $G^*$ and the majority example of Appendix~\ref{app:tunnels}. In each case the packing was checked to consist of induced $P_3$'s with pairwise disjoint pair sets and to be pair-maximal, and the optima and the structural claims were read off from the enumeration.

\paragraph{Randomised tests of lemmas.}
The lemmas of Sections~\ref{sec:optimal} and~\ref{sec:locality} and of Appendices~\ref{app:structure}--\ref{app:conflicts} were tested on random small graphs ($n\le10$; random, clustered, hub and sparse graphs; random and maximal packings) against all partitions of cost at most $|H|+3$, with code written independently of the proofs. No violation was found: the decomposition identity, Lemma~\ref{lem:half}, Theorem~\ref{thm:T1T2}, Corollary~\ref{cor:softfree}, Lemma~\ref{lem:near}, Lemmas~\ref{lem:EX} and~\ref{lem:NF}, Corollary~\ref{cor:cand} (inserted pairs had distance at most $2$ in every test), Theorem~\ref{thm:C1}, Corollary~\ref{cor:nf}, Lemmas~\ref{lem:HUB}, \ref{lem:SM}, \ref{lem:W1} and~\ref{lem:W2}, and Lemma~\ref{lem:D}. Control experiments in which a constant is weakened by one (for example $\alpha\le2\ell+c$ in Lemma~\ref{lem:HUB}, or $|C|\le\max\{D+u,2D,2u\}$ in Lemma~\ref{lem:SM}), or in which partitions of cost $\OPT+1$ or $\OPT+2$ are tested, produce violations, so the tests are not vacuous. The dynamic program of Theorem~\ref{thm:omega} was implemented and agreed with brute force on $2{,}778$ pairs of an instance ($n\le10$) and a budget $\ell$.
The checks belonging to Section~\ref{sec:soft} are listed in Appendix~\ref{app:soft}, those belonging to Section~\ref{sec:repack} at the end of Appendix~\ref{app:repack}, and those belonging to Section~\ref{sec:hardness} in Section~\ref{sec:hardcomp} and at the end of Appendix~\ref{app:hardness}; the latter include SAT-based exact solves of small instances of the reduction, in which UNSAT answers are proofs for the instance solved.
These tests are evidence for the statements; the proofs are the justification. The scripts are available from the author.
