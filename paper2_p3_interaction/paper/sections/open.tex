\section{Open problems}\label{sec:open}
The main question, whether CEaMP is fixed-parameter tractable in $\ell+w$, is answered negatively (Theorem~\ref{thm:hard}); the XP algorithms of Theorems~\ref{thm:A} and~\ref{thm:nl} are optimal up to the exact exponent of $n$. Positive results that remain are fixed-parameter tractability in $\ell$ for $w=0$~\cite{BFK18}, in $(\ell,s)$ for bounded components (Theorem~\ref{thm:K}), in $(\ell,D,\omega)$ (Theorem~\ref{thm:omega}), and for soft-triangle-free packings the reduction of the question to one hypothesis (Section~\ref{sec:soft}). What is open is the fine structure.

\begin{problem}[Hardness for bounded $c$; $c=2$]\label{op:boundc}\label{op:c2}
Is CEaMP W[1]-hard parameterized by $\ell$ when $c$ is bounded by a constant? In particular, is CEaMP with $c=2$ fixed-parameter tractable in $\ell$, or W[1]-hard? The reduction of Theorem~\ref{thm:hard} has $c=4p+2\ge26$ with $p\ge k^2+5=\ell+5$, because a blown-up clique of size $p$ cannot be split by an edit set of excess $\ell$ only if $p>\ell$ (Lemma~\ref{lem:hsplit}); the zero-excess semantics (Proposition~\ref{prop:hTR}) hold for every even $p\ge8$. What is missing is a gadget that holds a vertex of the chain together at bounded $c$ against $\ell$ edits. The complexity of $\ell=0$ for $3\le c\le22$ is open in~\cite{LPS23}, whose authors say a W[1]-hardness result for $c=2$ ``would also not be surprising''. For soft-triangle-free packings $\ell=0$ is polynomial (Corollary~\ref{cor:sfpoly}), so a hardness proof for $\ell=0$ at $c=2$ needs triangles in $I(H)$, hence $w\ge2$.

\emph{Bounded certificates at $\ell=0$, $c\le2$.} By Theorem~\ref{thm:MC0} (proved; finite classification by computer), at $\ell=0$ and $c\le2$ a bounded certificate always exists: if no zero-excess solution avoids remote edits, there is a certificate of at most $4$ remote pairs, found in time $f(w)n^{O(1)}$, and this holds with soft triangles. So the unbounded minimal certificates of Theorem~\ref{thm:CE} are not an obstruction to branching at $\ell=0$, $c\le2$; this does not decide the complexity at $c=2$ and $\ell\ge1$.
\end{problem}
\begin{problem}[Bounded certificates BC for $\ell\ge1$]\label{op:BC}\label{op:mincert}
Is there $K(\ell,D)$ such that a certificate of at most $K$ remote pairs always exists (for forests $I(H)$, soft-triangle-free packings, and $c\le2$)? Proved: $\ell=0$, $c\le2$ (Theorem~\ref{thm:MC0}) and $\ell=0$ for every $c$ in terms of the unit-lock Helly number (Theorem~\ref{thm:R0}); for $\ell\ge1$ only the sufficient criterion of Lemma~\ref{lem:mcD}, the pattern-wise statement of Lemma~\ref{lem:mcP}, and the obstruction to the natural weighted-gluing proof. \emph{Evidence only:} hill-climbing on forests gave minimum certificates $4,8,10,11,15$ for $\ell=0,\dots,4$ (below $4(\ell+1)$), and $12{,}000$ periodic chains with $c\le3$ at $\ell=0$ showed no growth. For $c\ge3$ with soft triangles even $\ell=0$ is open. For forests, BC and an oracle assumption imply fixed-parameter tractability in $\ell$ (Theorem~\ref{thm:F}).
\end{problem}
\begin{problem}[ETH-tight exponent]\label{op:eth}
Corollary~\ref{cor:hardeth} excludes $f(\ell,w)N^{o(\sqrt\ell)}$ (from $\ell=k^2$), while the algorithms run in time $N^{2\ell+O(1)}$ resp.\ $N^{\ell+O(1)}$ times $f(\ell,w)$. Is there an algorithm with exponent $O(\sqrt\ell)$, or is $N^{o(\ell)}$ excluded? A reduction with $\ell=O(k)$ would be needed for the latter.
\end{problem}
\begin{problem}[Base of the exponent]\label{op:seth}
For $\ell=0$, does a $(3-\varepsilon)^{w}n^{O(1)}$ algorithm exist, or is there a SETH-type lower bound? Theorem~\ref{thm:eth} gives only the exponent.
\end{problem}
\begin{problem}[$w=1$ with unbounded tree components]\label{op:forest}
If $I(H)$ is a forest with components of unbounded size, is CEaMP fixed-parameter tractable in $\ell$? For forests there is no soft triangle: $\ell=0$ is polynomial, the far-lift model is fixed-parameter tractable (Theorem~\ref{thm:U}), and the full problem is fixed-parameter tractable conditionally on BC and the oracle (O) (Theorem~\ref{thm:F}); the question is thus reduced to Open Problem~\ref{op:BC}.
\end{problem}
\begin{problem}[Soft triangles at $c\le2$]\label{op:tri2}
For $c\le2$ with triangles in $I(H)$, is $\Sigma$ always $2$-decomposable (equivalently, do lock cores of the tunnel model have at most two anchors)? Theorem~\ref{thm:MC0} proves the corresponding Helly property for the unit locks that matter for certificates; for the formulation of Remark~\ref{rem:tri2} only evidence is available; Example~\ref{ex:maj} shows that closure under the specific operation $m_3$ fails.
\end{problem}
\begin{problem}[Pointers of type T2 and deleted hard edges; reduction lemma; repacking]\label{op:T2}\label{op:del}\label{op:ML}
Can the far ends of inserted pairs of type T2 and of deleted hard edges between far members be handled in a dynamic program of width $g(\ell,w)$ on a restricted class (soft-triangle-free, bounded $c$)? For the full problem the answer is no unless $\mathrm{FPT}=\mathrm{W[1]}$, since such a program, a polynomial-time reduction lemma (given $(G,H,\ell)$ with $H$ pair-maximal, output NO correctly or an equivalent pair-maximal instance $(G^*,H^*,\ell^*)$ with $\ell^*\le\ell$, $w(H^*)\le f_1(\ell,w)$ and $\tw(\Gamma'(G^*,H^*))\le f_2(\ell,w)$), or a fixed-parameter algorithm \emph{locating} a repacking of bounded width and excess on YES instances (LOC, Open Problem~\ref{op:LOC}) would each give fixed-parameter tractability in $\ell+w$ (Proposition~\ref{prop:rpLOC}, Theorem~\ref{thm:omega}); by Theorem~\ref{thm:hard} none exists unless $\mathrm{FPT}=\mathrm{W[1]}$. Whether a merge pointer between two pieces joined by a soft pair can be forced at unbounded distance is open, as is the corrected normal form (Conjecture~\ref{conj:rpC1}).
\end{problem}
