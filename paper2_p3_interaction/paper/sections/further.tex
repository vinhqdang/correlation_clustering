\section{Further structure}\label{sec:further}
The results below describe optimal solutions, the components of $I(H)$, an $n^{\ell}$ algorithm, locality of inserted pairs, and repacking. They are all proved, in Appendices~\ref{app:structure}--\ref{app:repack}, and none is needed for the proofs in Sections~\ref{sec:csp}--\ref{sec:hardness}; the proofs are not repeated here. Unless said otherwise, $H$ is pair-maximal (for an arbitrary packing read the primed instance of Lemma~\ref{lem:MAX}), $S$ is an optimal solution of size at most $|H|+\ell$, $U$ is its set of $u\le\ell$ hard edits, $D\ge\max_v|Q(v)|$, and $Z=O(w+\ell)$. Appendix~\ref{app:status} tabulates for every result whether it holds for the input packing or for a pair-maximal extension.

\paragraph{Structural lemmas.}
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Twins} (Lemma~\ref{lem:twin}). If $u\ne v$ are adjacent with $N[u]=N[v]$, they lie in one cluster of every optimum, with no hypothesis on $H$.
\item \emph{Half-degree and diameter} (Lemma~\ref{lem:half}). In an optimal solution every vertex $v$ of a cluster $C$ has at least $(|C|-1)/2$ neighbours in $C$, and two non-adjacent vertices of $C$ have a common neighbour in $C$.
\item \emph{MAX} (Lemma~\ref{lem:MAX}, Section~\ref{sec:prelim}). Adding a maximal hard family to $H$ makes it pair-maximal and raises $w$ and $c$ by at most the size of the family, which is at most $\ell$ in a YES instance.
\item \emph{HUB$^+$} (Lemma~\ref{lem:HUB}). For any packing, in a YES instance, for every vertex $u$ the graph of hard non-edges on the hard neighbourhood $N_h(u)$ has matching number at most $\ell$, a vertex cover of size at most $2\ell$, and $\alpha(G[N_h(u)])\le2\ell+c+1$.
\item \emph{SM, small merges} (Lemma~\ref{lem:SM}). Let the \emph{pieces} of a solution be the components of the graph of its kept hard edges. If a cluster $C$ is the union of at least two pieces, then $|C|\le\max\{D+u+1,2D,2u\}$.
\item \emph{No edit inside the uncovered part} (Theorem~\ref{thm:C1}). Every cluster of an optimum meets $V\setminus V(H)$ in the empty set or in exactly one whole class of true twins, so no edit has both ends outside $V(H)$; edits with one end outside $V(H)$ cannot be excluded (Proposition~\ref{prop:c4}).
\item \emph{Normal form and candidates} (Lemma~\ref{lem:NF}, Corollary~\ref{cor:cand}). There are at most $2\ell$ clusters containing an end of a hard edit, each connected of size at most $2D+\ell+1$ and of bounded hard degree; one end of a hard edit determines at most $Z^2$ candidates for the other, which lie at distance at most $2$.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Components of $I(H)$ (fixed-parameter tractability in $(\ell,s)$).} Let $s$ be the maximum number of vertices spanned by a connected component of $I(H)$. CEaMP is solvable in time $1.62^{(\binom s2+1)\ell}n^{O(1)}+n3^s$ and has a kernel with at most $(s^2-s+2)\ell$ vertices (Theorem~\ref{thm:K}); in particular a perfect matching $I(H)$ is fixed-parameter tractable in $\ell$ (Corollary~\ref{cor:pm}). This is the reduction rule of van Bevern, Froese and Komusiewicz~\cite{BFK18} applied to components; the soundness of the rule, the algorithm of B\"ocker~\cite{Boe12} and the kernel of Chen and Meng~\cite{CM12} are cited, the rest is proved. For components of arbitrary size, after reducing tight reducible components at most $2\ell$ components remain (Theorem~\ref{thm:three}); what is left is a few arbitrarily large components (Open Problem~\ref{op:forest}). Since $s$ is not bounded by $w$ this is not a fixed-parameter algorithm in $\ell+w$.

\paragraph{An $n^{\ell}$ algorithm and a stronger parameter.} By Corollary~\ref{cor:cand} it suffices to guess one end of each hard edit: CEaMP is solvable in time $(n+1)^{\ell}Z_*^{2\ell}5^{w+\ell+1}n^{O(1)}$, $Z_*\le6w+9\ell+7$, for every packing (Theorem~\ref{thm:nl}), which halves the exponent of Theorem~\ref{thm:A}. Following clusters instead of guessing gives a fixed-parameter algorithm in $(\ell,D,\omega)$, where $\omega$ is the treewidth of the graph of edges and soft pairs outside the large hard components (Theorem~\ref{thm:omega}); $\omega$ is not bounded by any function of $(\ell,w)$ (Proposition~\ref{prop:expander}, from cubic expanders~\cite{GM09}), and is unbounded on the hard instances of Section~\ref{sec:hardness}.

\paragraph{Locality of inserted pairs (T1/T2).} Every inserted hard pair $ab$ of an optimum has a witness $z$ (a common neighbour of $a$ and $b$ in $G$ in their cluster), at least one of whose steps $az,zb$ is soft, and is of type T1 (both steps soft, owned by two distinct members through $z$; local in every tree decomposition of $I(H)$, Proposition~\ref{prop:bag}) or of type T2 (one step a kept hard edge) (Theorem~\ref{thm:T1T2}). Merges of two pieces joined by no soft pair are of type T1 only (Corollary~\ref{cor:softfree}); merges joined by a soft pair can be of type T2 (instances with $n=7$ and $n=8$, Proposition~\ref{prop:cex}). For every $m\ge3$ there is an instance with $w=1$, $c=2$, $\ell=1$ in which every optimum deletes a hard edge whose ends lie in packed $P_3$'s at distance $m-1$ in $I(H)$ (Proposition~\ref{prop:FAR}, the ``FAR family'').

\paragraph{Repacking.} The parameters belong to the packing: exchanging one packed $P_3$ for an induced $P_3$ keeps $|H|$ and raises $w$ by at most one, and a pair-maximal repair costs at most $\ell$ more (Lemma~\ref{lem:rpex}). Every hard edit of an optimum lies in an induced $P_3$ and is bridged (local in every decomposition) or absorbable by one exchange (Lemma~\ref{lem:rpwit}); no hard edit has two uncovered ends (Lemma~\ref{lem:rpunc}).
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{The FAR artifact.} The FAR family has intrinsic excess one, but after one exchange $I(H^*)$ is a cycle and its only hard edit is at an uncovered vertex; the spine family has excess zero and $\tw(I(H^*))\le2$ after one exchange and one augmentation (Propositions~\ref{prop:rpFAR} and~\ref{prop:rpSp}).
\item \emph{PL, a one-sided algorithm.} In time $5^{w+1}(Z^2+1)^{3\ell(w+1)}n^{O(1)}$ one computes an upper bound on $\OPT$ that is exact whenever some optimum has only hard edits that are co-bagged or at local hubs (Theorem~\ref{thm:rpPL}); the promise cannot be tested.
\item \emph{Theorem N.} No polynomial procedure outputs a repacking with excess and width bounded in $\ell$ alone unless $\mathrm P=\mathrm{NP}$ (and, under ETH, none with $\tw\le|H|^{1-\varepsilon}$) (Theorem~\ref{thm:rpN}); bounded-radius augmentation cannot find the good packing (Proposition~\ref{prop:rpaug}); the naive normal form ``hard edits have an uncovered end'' is false (Proposition~\ref{prop:rpnf}), and a corrected normal form is open (Conjecture~\ref{conj:rpC1}).
\item \emph{Locating the repacking} in fixed-parameter time is impossible unless $\mathrm{FPT}=\mathrm{W[1]}$, since it would give fixed-parameter tractability in $\ell+w$ (Proposition~\ref{prop:rpLOC} and Corollary~\ref{cor:rpLOCno}, with Theorem~\ref{thm:hard}).
\end{itemize}
