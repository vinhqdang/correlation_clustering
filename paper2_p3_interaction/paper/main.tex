\begin{abstract}
Li, Pilipczuk and Sorge study Cluster Editing parameterized by the excess $\ell$ of the solution size over a modification-disjoint packing $H$ of induced $P_3$'s.
We ask which interaction structure of $H$ makes this parameterization tractable, and use the treewidth $w$ of the intersection graph $I(H)$ of the packing as the measure.
We prove: (i) for $\ell=0$ the problem is a CSP with domain size $3$ whose constraint scopes are cliques of $I(H)$, hence solvable in $3^{w+1}n^{O(1)}$ given a tree decomposition;
(ii) for every $\ell$ it is solvable in $7^{w+1}n^{2\ell+O(1)}$ given a tree decomposition (XP in $\ell$, FPT in $w$), and domain size $7$ cannot be lowered in the natural encoding;
(iii) under ETH there is no $2^{o(\mathrm{tw}(I(H)))}n^{O(1)}$ algorithm even for $\ell=0$ (exponent only, no base);
(iv) $w$ alone does not suffice (NP-hard for $w=0$ with unbounded $\ell$), and $\mathrm{tw}(G)$ and $\mathrm{tw}(I(H))$ are incomparable, with a tradeoff $\ell+\mathrm{tw}(I(H))\ge 2m-2$ on stars.
The main question, whether the problem is fixed-parameter tractable in $\ell+w$, is \emph{open}; we do not prove or disprove it.
Further statements obtained by our team are listed separately as unaudited claims, and are not part of the results above.
\end{abstract}
